# My Personal Project
I want to build a sort of <strong>"distraction tracker"</strong>, what will happen is that the user upon opening the app/chrome extension can <em><strong>input multiple websites</strong></em> they wish to stay out of of <strong>(eg. Youtube, Instagram)</strong>. once inputed, the app will <em><strong>track the current website the user is on</em></strong>, if the user goes onto a website that was listed in the distraction ListArray by the user and prompt a <em><strong>popup saying "stay focused on task!"</em></strong>. I also hope to implement a sort of points system, where there can be a <em><strong>streak of studying time </em></strong>without being distracted and once you hit a certain streak you can <em><strong>rank up (eg. gold, diamond etc)</em></strong>. you can also set a <em><strong>time limit </em></strong>on how long you want the app to track you. 

## Who will use it? 
My goal is to cater towards people who cant on task for more than 30 minutes (aka me)

## Why is this project of interest to you?
I am a easily distracted person and often find myself straying away from what im supposed to be doing for hours sometimes, hence wasting my productivity in the task, in fact i am quite distracted writing this right now. i hope with this app, i can improve my focus on a task and hand and improve my producivity
